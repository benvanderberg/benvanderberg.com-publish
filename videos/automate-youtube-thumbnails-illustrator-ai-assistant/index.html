Automate YouTube Thumbnails in Illustrator Beta with AI Assistant (Bulk Variations + Skills)

October 5, 2026

Use AI Assistant in Adobe Illustrator Beta to prepare presenter portraits, generate YouTube thumbnail variations from a spreadsheet, and export files organized by speaker.

Creating dozens of YouTube title card variations by hand is tedious. This demo shows how AI Assistant in Adobe Illustrator Beta automates the process, from preparing presenter portraits to generating bulk thumbnail variations from a CSV spreadsheet.

See how to convert speaker photos to black-and-white portraits with their backgrounds removed, save the workflow as a reusable Skill, and generate thumbnails with matching backgrounds, presenter images, and product icons. The final files are exported and organized into folders by speaker.
