Adobe Photoshop and Generative Fill to customize images for ads

August 11, 2024

How to leverage Photoshop's Generative Fill alongside Adobe Firefly to produce customized advertising images efficiently.

A walkthrough showing how Photoshop’s Generative Fill, powered by Adobe Firefly, can be used to create and customize images for advertising campaigns. The video demonstrates selecting regions of a photo and using AI to swap backgrounds, add props, or reframe compositions to suit different ad formats. This technique helps creative teams quickly produce multiple ad variants from a single source image.
