Use search bar in Chrome to generate images with Adobe Firefly

January 16, 2026

Generate Adobe Firefly images directly from the Chrome browser search bar without opening a separate app.

This video shows how to trigger Adobe Firefly image generation directly from Chrome’s address bar without opening a separate tab or app. The demo covers enabling the integration in Chrome settings and typing a prompt into the omnibar to generate images on the fly — a quick way to ideate without breaking your flow.
