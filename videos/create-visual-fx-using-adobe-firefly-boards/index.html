Create Visual FX using Adobe Firefly Boards

September 19, 2025

Use Adobe Firefly Boards to generate and compose visual effects for creative and motion projects.

This video explores how Adobe Firefly Boards can be used as a creative workspace for generating and assembling visual effects. The demo walks through prompting Firefly to produce VFX elements and organizing them on a Board for iteration and refinement. It’s a great look at how generative AI can speed up the early stages of visual effects production.
